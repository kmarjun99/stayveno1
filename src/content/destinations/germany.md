    a: A Wohngemeinschaft (WG) is a shared flat where you rent a private room and share the kitchen and bathroom with other tenants. It is the most common and affordable student setup in Germany, and a fast way to meet people. If you would rather have your own kitchen and bathroom, filter for a studio instead.

Student housing operators — private halls and managed WG buildings — are the most dependable route for an international student, because they are set up for tenants who arrive without a local rental history.
