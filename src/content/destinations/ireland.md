  - q: What is the difference between a licence and a tenancy?
    a: Most Irish student accommodation is offered under a licence agreement, not a residential tenancy. That affects your notice period, your deposit rights and how disputes are handled. It is not necessarily worse, but it is different — we walk you through the specific clauses in your agreement before you commit.
