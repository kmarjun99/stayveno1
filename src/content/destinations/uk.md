The UK takes more international students than anywhere in Europe, and the housing market around its universities moves fast. Purpose-built student accommodation — private halls run by operators rather than the university — is the most reliable option for someone booking from abroad, because the contracts are standardised and the buildings are managed.

Every UK listing on Stay Veno is a room we have confirmed is available for your dates, with a provider that is a registered business at a real address, tenancy terms we have read, and a current video tour. We tell you the all-in weekly price, whether it includes contents insurance and a gym, and how long the walk to your campus actually is.

If your visa decision is still pending, ask us to prioritise providers with a visa-refusal cancellation clause so your deposit is protected if things do not go to plan.
