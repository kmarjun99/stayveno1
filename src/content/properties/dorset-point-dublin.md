A large, well-run student building on Dorset Street, a straight 16-minute walk into Trinity College and close to the bus and Luas lines for DCU and TU Dublin. Rooms are en-suite in flats of six, with study rooms, a gym and a cinema room downstairs.

Dublin's student housing is scarce and priced accordingly, so a confirmed en-suite this central is worth moving on. Choose the academic-year licence or the 51-week term — we show the total for each, not just the weekly rate.
