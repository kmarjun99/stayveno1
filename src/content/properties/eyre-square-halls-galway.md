An en-suite room beside Eyre Square, in the middle of Galway and next to the bus and train stations. The University of Galway is a flat 20-minute walk or a short cycle along the river; ATU is a bus ride to the north of the city.

Galway is small, walkable and sociable, and this is about as central as student accommodation gets. Flats of four share a kitchen; all bills and Wi-Fi are included, with academic-year and 51-week licences available.
