A self-contained studio on College Street, a ten-minute walk from the University of Toronto's St. George campus and close to the streetcar for TMU and OCAD. You get your own bathroom and kitchenette; the gym, study lounges and rooftop room are shared.

Heating is included — worth having from December to March — along with the other bills and Wi-Fi. The provider accepts international students on an offer letter and proof of funds, with no Canadian co-signer needed. Fall-term and full-year contracts are both available.
