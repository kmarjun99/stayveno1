A well-run big building by Stratford station, where the Elizabeth line, Jubilee line and Overground put Queen Mary in nine minutes and central London campuses in under twenty. You get a private en-suite room and share a modern kitchen with four other students.

Stratford is one of the smartest value choices in London: the rent is a long way below zone 1, but the commute is short and everything — Westfield, the Olympic Park, supermarkets — is on your doorstep. Bills, Wi-Fi, gym and cinema room are included.
