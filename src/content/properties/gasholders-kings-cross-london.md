A self-contained studio a short canal-side walk from UCL, King's Cross and St Pancras, in one of the best-connected spots in London. You get your own bathroom and kitchenette, so nothing is shared except the study rooms, gym and rooftop terrace.

The building is aimed squarely at postgraduates and international students who want to arrive, unpack and start their course without a housemate lottery. Bills, Wi-Fi and contents insurance are all in the weekly price, and reception is staffed around the clock.
