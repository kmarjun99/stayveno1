A furnished studio in Friedrichshain, a few minutes from Warschauer Straße and its U-Bahn, S-Bahn and tram connections. You have your own kitchenette and bathroom; the roof terrace and co-working lounge are shared. Humboldt is about 20 minutes by train, TU Berlin a little more.

The weekly figure is warm rent — heating and service charges are included — with electricity and internet already set up. The provider issues the landlord confirmation you need to register your address, and accepts a blocked account as proof of funds.
