Right on Oxford Road, the spine that runs through Manchester's student quarter, this building puts both universities, the hospitals and the library within a ten-minute walk. Rooms are en-suite, in flats of six, with a big shared kitchen and study rooms downstairs.

Manchester is one of the best-value major UK student cities, and this is a central, well-managed option with everything included in the weekly price. The bus corridor outside your door runs late into the night if your campus or placement is further out.
