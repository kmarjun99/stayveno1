A private room in a four-person shared flat (WG) in Dahlem, the quiet, green district that Freie Universität calls home. Campus is a nine-minute walk. You get your own furnished bedroom and share the kitchen with three others and a bathroom with one.

A WG is the standard, affordable way students live in Germany and the quickest way to meet people when you arrive alone. Warm rent covers heating; the provider supports your address registration and accepts a blocked account for the visa.
